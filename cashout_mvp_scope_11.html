Fired on successful cash outs only. Cancelled requests (price changed, market suspended) do not affect liabilities and are not logged at MVP.

FieldTypeDescription

C Why only settled events
Settled only feeds the Ticker. A successful cashout changes the book's liability immediately — Trading needs to see it.
Cancelled requests are not shown. Price changed or suspended during validation = no money moved, no liability change, no trading action required.
Sharp activity signal. A large winning bet cashing out at >80% bet change signals the customer expects the outcome — useful for Trading to adjust limits or monitor the market.

index=sportsbook sourcetype=cashout event_type=cashout_settled earliest=-24h
| stats sum(liability_released) as total_liability_released, sum(cashout_amount_paid) as total_paid, count as num_cashouts by sport
| sort -total_liability_released
